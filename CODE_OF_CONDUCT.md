# Code of Conduct

Pix follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, and make this a place where a first-time contributor (or a first-time programmer) feels welcome.

- Welcome questions, including beginner ones.
- Disagree about ideas, never about people.
- No harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- No sharing anyone's private information without permission.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes taking part uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, [@LED-esma](https://github.com/LED-esma), on GitHub. Reports are kept private. The maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions that break this code, and may ban people who keep doing so.
