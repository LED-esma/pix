# Changelog

## 0.1.2 (2026-10-07)

- **Automatic updates**: Pix checks daily while it runs, gets new versions ready in the background (signed by the same developer, or not installed), and swaps them in while it's idle. Afterwards the card says "Updated to Pix …" with What's New. **Update Automatically** in Settings turns it off; then the blob peeks out once and the card offers **Update** when a version is out.
- Updates can't leave you without Pix: the old app is kept until the new one is in place.
- **Check for Updates** says "Pix is up to date" when it is.
- First-run steps (Pick your AI, the welcome card, adding an AI) stay open when you click another app.
- When you undo every change from an answer, the answer is marked **Undone**.
- The right-click menu is shorter: Permissions, Open at Login and Hide Pix are gone from it (the first two live in Settings; Hiding covers the third).

## 0.1.1 (2026-10-07)

- **Pick your AI** on first open: every AI your Mac can use, each with what it's best at, cost, where your words go and speed. Claude is picked when you're signed in, otherwise the free built-in AI.
- Picking Claude when it isn't installed: Pix installs Claude Code, then opens the Claude plan sign-in in your browser (no Terminal menu).
- The card's AI menu uses the same plain words ("Claude — best at doing things, uses your plan").

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-07): first public release

The first open-source release of Pix.

- **Works the second you install it**: on Apple Intelligence Macs, Pix answers with the AI built into macOS; on other Macs, **Get Free AI** downloads one that runs on your Mac. No keys, no accounts.
- **Pix's own engine** runs every free AI (built-in, on your Mac, any AI service) without anything else installed; Claude plans run through Claude Code.
- **Math the built-in AI can't get wrong**: it sets up the steps, Pix works out every number.
- **Your Mac**: Reminders, Calendar, Notes, Shortcuts, timers, scheduled questions, files (organize, move, zip, duplicates), settings, windows, all with Undo.
- **Your apps and the web**: Do It (Pix clicks and types) and Show Me (Pix flies over and points); pages in Safari and Chrome read as text; visual apps by picture on Claude.
- **Explaining**: walkthroughs with real math, live graphs, physics simulations and diagrams on a pop-up board.
- **Talking**: hold the shortcut or click the mic; optional "Hey Pix"; spoken answers in the most natural installed voice.
- **Safety**: irreversible actions always ask; protected folders can't be moved; text in files and pages is treated as data.
- **Universal**: one app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 15 and later.
